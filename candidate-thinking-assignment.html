Thinking assignment

Show us how you would run KlimaShift smarter

We cut energy waste in commercial buildings. Read how we work, then tell us what to automate, how we would know it works, and what to do first.

What we do

KlimaShift helps restaurant, café and retail chains stop wasting electricity.

🔌

Fit a small kit

A meter with current clamps on the electrical panel, plus temperature sensors and controls on key equipment.

📊

Stream the data

Our platform shows waste: equipment running overnight, freezers short-cycling, poor power factor. It also flags equipment likely to fail.

💰

Charge for results

Customers pay for the hardware and a monthly subscription, and expect a lower bill.

The catch: we win or lose on whether our savings claims and alerts are correct. That is why "how do we know it works?" matters as much as "what can we automate?"

How the work runs, end to end

Seven steps from first conversation to renewal.

1WinResearch chains, audits, pilots, proposals
2BuildAssemble kits, ship to cities
3InstallField electricians, often at night
4ConnectEach meter mapped to the right equipment
5AnalyseDetect waste, estimate savings, predict failures
6ActSchedules, controls, maintenance
7ProveSavings reports and renewal
Today: a small team runs most of this with spreadsheets, chat messages and manual judgment.

Your assignment

One document, up to 8 pages, answering five questions. Diagrams welcome.

1Map it

Where does work get stuck, get repeated, or depend on one person?

  • A diagram
  • A ranked list
  • How you decided the ranking

2Decide what to automate

Pick the 5 things most worth automating or handing to AI.

For each of the 5

  • What it does
  • Which data it needs
  • What stays human
  • What happens if it is wrong

Also name 2 you would NOT automate yet

  • And explain why not

3Design the evals

For each of your 5, how will we know it works?

  • What "correct" means
  • Where ground truth comes from
  • Metrics and thresholds
  • How failures are caught after launch
  • Who reviews edge cases

Then go deeper on one: sketch its eval in enough detail that someone could build it.

4Prioritise

You have 3 people and 90 days, and everything looks important. How do you choose what comes first, what waits and what gets dropped?

CoverWhat we want to see
Your criteriaFor example customer impact, risk, effort, data readiness, learning value, and how you weigh them
One real trade-offWhat you would choose, and who it would disappoint
What would change your mindNew information that would reorder the list
An urgent interruptionA customer or founder asks for something that breaks the plan. What do you do?

5Sequence it

Turn your priorities into a 90-day plan.

Day 14A first visible win
Day 30Early results and the first lessons
Day 60Scale what worked, cut what did not
Day 90Proof, using measures you named upfront

Also say what you would need from us, and which assumptions you are least sure of.
